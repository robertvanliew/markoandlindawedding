# Home (`/`)

Scope: the single public page: hero, When & Where, RSVP, footer. Mode: Persuade (the guest decides and acts: RSVP).

Audience and job: invited guests, mostly on a phone from a text message, possibly older; read the details, then RSVP within their seat cap. Proof/content: the invitation wording and facts only (PRODUCT.md). Constraints: brief §4–§6; floral art cropped from `Invitation/linda-wedding.png` until the designer's files arrive. Craft reference (structure, not look): thevanschatz.com, the same developer's earlier wedding site.

Memorable moment: the embossed peonies at the screen's corners unfold on load, then sway slowly as if in a light breeze.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The whole site is the invitation's world, not a picture of the invitation: full-bleed ivory page, peonies framing the screen itself. Refuses the card-on-a-page presentation and the stock wedding template (photo hero, countdown, icon tiles).

OWN-WORLD: Ivory paper (#fdfbf8) warming to a soft ground (#f7f1eb) with faint blush glows; taupe-umber ink (#8a6a4f names, #7a5b41 body, #5e4430 headings and buttons); embossed white peonies in two screen corners; Pinyon Script for the couple's names only; Cormorant Garamond italic for section titles and lead lines; Jost for body, labels and buttons. Soft rounded cards (28px) with low warm shadows, pill buttons, rounded fields.

STORY: Guest lands inside the invitation, reads names, date and both venues, taps RSVP (always one tap away in the sticky bar), finds their party by name, answers within their reserved seats, gets a calm confirmation.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sticky ivory bar (script "Linda & Marko" left; When & Where, RSVP links and a filled "Reply now" pill right). Full-screen hero: peonies bleeding in from top-left and bottom-right screen corners; centred "Together with their families", the names in large script, the italic invitation line, a gradient hairline, the date in Cormorant caps, both venue names, then a filled RSVP pill beside a ghost "See the details" pill and the reply-by date. All visible at 390x844 and 1440x900.

FORM: The invitation's world as a modern single-page wedding site (user-pinned). seed: none (pinned) — user: "the website needs to look like thevanschatz.com with the flowers on the outside of the home page instead of the flyer! The website should look like the invitation as whole not visitors seeing an invitation as the website".

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
